# Task 2 组合拳实战演示：句式 × 同义替换 × Points
### 从"骨架"到"成品段落"的完整流水线

> **本文件只演示 Task 2。** Task 1 的演示见 `ielts-writing-task1` skill 的 worked-examples.md。
>
> 这份文件回答一个问题：**背了骨架之后，实际怎么组装？**
>
> 每一组演示包含四层：
> `① 骨架` → `② 替换槽` → `③ Points 填入` → `④ 成品段落（可直接背的成品）`
> 最后附 `⑤ 反例诊断`：同一个骨架用错会变成什么样。
>

> **配套**：句式骨架见 `patterns.md`；词汇与论点见 `vocabulary.md`。

## 演示 1｜Task 2 Agree–Disagree：「政府是否应免费提供大学教育」

**题目**
> According to some people, students from all economic backgrounds should be able to attend university. They believe that the government should provide free university education for everyone. Do you agree with this view?

### ① 骨架（四段式）

```
引言：背景改写句 + 立场句（含笼统理由）
Body1：主题句（理由1）+ 机制 + 例证 + 后果
Body2：主题句（理由2）+ 机制 + 例证 + 让步反驳
结论：重述立场 + 概括理由
```

### ② 替换槽

```
背景改写骨架：The question of whether or not {题干从句} has been the subject of {recent/heated} public {discussion/debate}.
  题干从句 = the government should {make tertiary education freely available to all learners}
  替换 tertiary education: higher education / university education / post-secondary study
  替换 freely available to: free of charge for / funded by the state for / accessible at no cost to

立场骨架：{Higher education}, in my opinion, should be free regardless of {income}, on the grounds that it would benefit {both individuals and society}.
  替换 on the grounds that: for the simple reason that / because / since
  替换 benefit both individuals and society: serve the interests of both the individual and the wider community
```

### ③ Points 填入（从 Points 银行取）

| 位置 | Points | 来源角度 |
|---|---|---|
| 理由 1 | 许多学生无力支付；即使能付也要贷款+兼职 → 影响成绩与职业发展 | 公平 / 个人发展 |
| 理由 2 | 教育人口是国家的长期投资；没有受过教育的劳动力，国家无法繁荣 | 经济 / 长期 vs 短期 |
| 例证 1 | 假设情境：`If a student has to work full-time while studying, their grades are likely to suffer.` | 假设情境路径 |
| 例证 2 | 一般化：`In many developing countries, families still cannot afford tertiary education.` | 一般化实例路径 |
| 让步 | 承认财政负担（成本论），但用"不作为的代价更高"反击 | 反方四形态之① |

### ④ 成品段落

**Introduction**
> The question of whether or not the government should make tertiary education freely available to all learners has been the subject of heated public debate. Higher education, in my opinion, should be free regardless of income, on the grounds that it would benefit both individuals and society.

**Body 1**
> Firstly, one argument in favour of eliminating university fees is that many learners are unable to pay for their education, despite a strong desire to obtain degrees. Without sufficient financial support, these students have no way of fulfilling their ambitions, and even those who can afford the fees often have to take out loans and work part-time to cover their living costs. This can severely affect their grades and, in turn, limit their career prospects. If a student has to work full-time while studying, for example, their academic performance is likely to suffer.

**Body 2**
> A further reason why university should be state-funded is that educating the population represents a long-term investment in the future of any country. No country can prosper without an educated workforce, since skilled graduates are the people who drive innovation and economic growth. In many developing countries, families still cannot afford tertiary education, and as a result a great deal of talent is simply wasted. Admittedly, funding free university places would place considerable strain on public finances; however, the cost of leaving that talent undeveloped would be far higher in the long run.

**Conclusion**
> In conclusion, I firmly believe that higher education should be free for all students. This is mainly because it removes an unfair financial barrier and because an educated population benefits the whole country.

### ⑤ 反例诊断

**错误版本**
> There are those who argue that students should be able to attend university, and therefore the government should provide free education for everyone.

**病因**：`There are those who argue that` 用来包装一个**题目已经陈述过的事实性前提**，语义错配 —— 这个结构应该用于"存在争议的观点"，而不是复述题干。同时该句过度泛化，看不出作者立场。
**处方**：改写句只负责"陈述议题"，立场交给第二句。

## 演示 2｜Task 2 Discuss Both Views：「远程办公的利弊」改造为双边讨论

**题目**
> Some people believe that employees should work from home, while others think that working in an office is better. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### ① 骨架

```
引言：双方对立背景句 + "本文先看 A，再看 B，最后说明立场"
Body1：View A 的最强理由 + 机制 + 例证
Body2：View B 的最强理由 + 机制 + 例证 + 我的判断
结论：重述双方 + 明确站边
```

### ② 替换槽

```
背景骨架：While some people argue that {立场 A}, others maintain that {立场 B}.
  立场 A = employees should be allowed to work from home
  替换 argue: believe / claim / contend / are of the opinion
  替换 maintain: insist / hold / point out

讨论骨架 1：Those who support {观点 A} argue that {论点}.
讨论骨架 2：From the perspective of {群体}, {观点}.
  群体: employees / employers / working parents / city planners

站边骨架：While {观点 A} has considerable merit, {观点 B} is more persuasive, since {理由}.
```

### ③ Points 填入

| 位置 | Points |
|---|---|
| View A 理由 | 省通勤时间与成本 + 更灵活照顾家庭 → 生活质量与效率提升 |
| View A 例证 | 假设：`If a parent can collect their children from school, they are less likely to leave the workforce.` |
| View B 理由 | 面对面协作产生创意与信任 + 新人更快成长 → 长期生产力更高 |
| View B 例证 | 一般化：`In many creative industries, teams still rely on spontaneous conversations in the office.` |
| 我的判断 | 混合模式最优，但**必须明确站边**：在需要协作的行业，办公室仍不可替代 |

### ④ 成品段落

**Introduction**
> While some people argue that employees should be allowed to work from home, others maintain that the traditional office remains essential. This essay will examine both positions before explaining why I believe a degree of office attendance is still necessary.

**Body 1**
> Those who support remote working argue that it saves employees both time and money. Commuting is expensive and time-consuming, and the hours saved can be redirected towards work or family life. From the perspective of working parents, this flexibility is particularly valuable: if a parent can collect their children from school, they are far less likely to leave the workforce altogether. For many households, therefore, home working is not a luxury but a practical necessity.

**Body 2**
> Supporters of office-based work, on the other hand, point out that collaboration depends on proximity. Ideas are often generated in spontaneous conversations rather than scheduled meetings, and new employees learn far more quickly when they can observe experienced colleagues at close range. In many creative industries, teams still rely on these informal exchanges, and no video call fully reproduces them. While remote working clearly has considerable merit, I find the case for the office more persuasive, since most organisations ultimately depend on the trust and creativity that face-to-face contact generates.

**Conclusion**
> In conclusion, although home working offers undeniable flexibility, the collaborative benefits of the office should not be underestimated. In my view, the most sensible arrangement combines the two.

## 演示 3｜同义替换的"三级火箭"

同一句话的三种档次，考场上按剩余时间和把握度选择。

**要表达的意思**：手机让人们的沟通更方便，但也让人面对面交流变少。

| 档位 | 写法 | 用到的骨架/替换 |
|---|---|---|
| 🅐 6.5 保底 | Mobile phones make communication easier, but people talk face to face less than before. | 简单并列，准确无错 |
| 🅑 7.5 主力 | Mobile phones have made communication considerably more convenient, although they have reduced the amount of face-to-face contact between people. | `have made X + 比较级` / `although` 让步 / `the amount of` 名词化 |
| 🅒 8+ 加分 | While mobile phones have undoubtedly made communication more convenient, this convenience has come at the expense of face-to-face interaction. | `undoubtedly` 强调 / `come at the expense of` 高阶搭配 / 分词与名词化 |

**考场策略**：主体段的前两句用 🅑，中间解释用 🅐（保证准确），全篇留 1–2 处 🅒（点缀，不冒险）。

## 演示 4｜Points 银行的"跨话题搬运"

**同一个骨架，三个话题，同一套 Points 逻辑**

骨架：`{X} places a heavy burden on {Y}, which in turn {Z}.`

| 话题 | X | Y | Z | 意译 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 老龄化 | An ageing population | public healthcare systems | reduces the funding available for education | 老龄人口加重医疗负担 → 挤压教育经费 |
| 城市化 | Rapid urbanisation | local infrastructure | leads to congestion and housing shortages | 快速城市化加重基础设施负担 → 拥堵与住房短缺 |
| 过度旅游 | Mass tourism | fragile ecosystems | threatens biodiversity in the long term | 大众旅游加重生态负担 → 威胁生物多样性 |

**做法**：把 `{X} places a heavy burden on {Y}` 当作"万用插头"，左边的 X 换成任何话题名词，右边的 Z 换成 Points 银行里的"第三层影响"，一句话就能成立。这就是"跨话题都能用"的组合拳核心。

## 演示 5｜从零到 290 词的 40 分钟流水线（时间戳版）

以「Some people think that governments should spend money on public transport rather than on new roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree?」为例。

```
00:00–03:00  拆题
  Topic = public transport vs new roads（交通投资）
  Task  = To what extent do you agree or disagree（必须表态且一致）
  决定立场：同意"优先公共交通"（理由好写：环保 + 公平）

03:00–07:00  用 Points 银行掘金（不写整句，只写关键词）
  理由1：公交投资 → 减排 → 改善空气质量 → 公共健康支出下降（环境 + 公共财政）
  理由2：公交便宜 → 低收入群体也能通勤 → 就业机会公平（公平 + 经济）
  让步 ：新建道路确实缓解拥堵，但诱导更多私家车 → 长期无效（反方四形态之③效果论）
  例证 ：`In many large cities, expanding roads has simply encouraged more car use.`（常识共识）

07:00–12:00  Introduction（2 句）
  The question of whether governments should prioritise public transport over new road
  construction has been the subject of heated debate. In my opinion, investment in public
  transport is the more sensible use of public funds, for two main reasons.

12:00–24:00  Body 1
  Firstly, one argument in favour of funding public transport is that it reduces both
  congestion and air pollution. ...（机制）... As a result, ...（后果）...
  In many large cities, ...（例证）...

24:00–33:00  Body 2
  A further reason why ... is that reliable public transport widens access to employment. ...
  Admittedly, ... However, ...（让步反驳）

33:00–36:00  Conclusion（2 句）
  In conclusion, ... This is mainly because ... and because ...

36:00–39:00  自检 7 问（见主指南 10.3）
39:00–40:00  数词数（目标 265–290）
```

**成品骨架对照（写完后回看骨架，确认每句都归位）**

| 段落 | 句数 | 用的骨架 |
|---|---|---|
| Introduction | 2 | T2-1 骨架1 + T2-2 骨架3 |
| Body 1 | 5 | T2-4 骨架1 + T2-5 骨架2 + T2-6 骨架5 + T2-7 骨架7 + T2-6 骨架1 |
| Body 2 | 5 | T2-4 骨架1 + T2-5 骨架6 + T2-7 骨架10 + T2-8 骨架1 |
| Conclusion | 2 | T2-12 骨架1 + 概括理由句 |

> 这份流水线上的每一个动作，都可直接复制到任何 Task 2 题目。**骨架固定、Points 随题变形**，这就是"组合拳"的全部秘密。
