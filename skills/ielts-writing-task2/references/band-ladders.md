# Task 2 阶梯式修改范例

本文件展示怎样保留学生立场和主要观点，逐步修正 Task Response、组织展开和语言控制。分数只表示可能区间，不能替代对完整限时作文的正式评分。

使用时只读取与当前题型对应的一组。不要把最终版本交给学生背诵。真正要学习的是每一轮修改的判断：

1. 先确认是否回答全部指令，立场是否清楚，观点是否相关并得到支持。
2. 再调整段落中心、论证顺序和句间关系。
3. 最后修正语法、搭配和表达精度，不用生僻词掩盖内容问题。

## 范例一：Agree or Disagree

### 题目

以下训练题为本项目自拟。

> Some people believe that all public transport should be free in order to reduce traffic congestion. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

### 学生原稿

> Nowadays, traffic jam is a serious problem in many city. Some people believe public transport should free for everybody. I totally agree with this opinion because it can help poor people and reduce cars.
>
> Firstly, many people cannot afford the transport fee every day. If bus and train are free, they can go to work more easy and save much money. This is good for poor families. For example, a worker may spend a lot of their salary on taking bus, so free transport can reduce their burden.
>
> Secondly, free public transport will solve congestion. People will not drive cars because bus is no cost. Therefore, roads have less vehicles and citizens can arrive faster. Also, environment will become better because there are less pollution.
>
> In conclusion, I agree that governments should provide free public transport. It benefits citizens and solve the traffic problem.

### 初步诊断

如果其余表现相近，这份原稿可能受到以下限制：

- `TR-DEV`：两个理由相关，但第二段把“免费”直接等同于“放弃开车”，中间机制缺失。
- `TR-DEV`：`solve congestion` 和 `environment will become better` 都过度断言，没有限定条件。
- `CC-LINK`：段落顺序清楚，但 `Firstly / Secondly / Therefore / Also` 主要承担表面连接，论证推进较薄。
- `LR-COLL`：`traffic jam` 泛指时通常用复数或 `traffic congestion`；`bus is no cost` 不自然。
- `GRA-AGR` 与 `GRA-V`：`many city`、`should free`、`less vehicles`、`solve` 等错误重复出现。

### 第一轮：只修 Task Response

保留学生“完全同意”的立场，但把两个理由写成可论证的判断：

> I agree that making public transport free could reduce congestion because it would make regular travel more affordable and give some drivers a practical reason to leave their cars at home. However, the policy would work only where buses and trains are frequent and reliable.

扩展第二个理由的机制：

> Price is one factor in commuters' decisions. If buses and trains cost nothing while driving still involves fuel and parking charges, some people who currently travel by car may switch to public transport. Even a modest shift could reduce the number of vehicles entering busy city centres at peak times. Free fares alone would not remove congestion, but they could form part of an effective transport policy when services are reliable.

这一轮改变了什么：

- 立场仍然支持免费公共交通。
- 把 `will solve congestion` 改成有条件的因果关系。
- 没有强行加入完整反方段落，只承认服务质量会影响政策效果。
- 删除未经展开的环境论点，避免一段同时塞入拥堵和污染两个中心。

### 第二轮：只修组织和展开

两段分别承担不同功能：

- Body 1：可负担性怎样改善就业和家庭预算。
- Body 2：价格差怎样影响通勤选择，并在什么条件下减少车辆。

修改后的 Body 1：

> The first benefit would be greater access to employment. Daily fares take up a noticeable share of a low-paid worker's income, particularly when several family members commute. Removing this cost would allow people to accept jobs farther from home without losing much of their earnings to travel. This matters most where affordable housing is far from major employment centres. For a household with two working adults, the savings could instead help pay for food, rent or school expenses. The policy would therefore help household budgets while widening the area in which residents could look for work.

段落没有使用虚构百分比，也没有把“省钱”重复三次。每句都向就业机会这一结果推进。

### 第三轮：修语言并形成完整版本

> Traffic congestion places a heavy cost on many cities, and free public transport is often proposed as a way to reduce car use. I agree with this policy because it would improve access to work and make buses and trains more attractive than driving, provided that the services were reliable.
>
> The first benefit would be greater access to employment. Daily fares take up a noticeable share of a low-paid worker's income, particularly when several family members commute. Removing this cost would allow people to accept jobs farther from home without losing much of their earnings to travel. This matters most where affordable housing is far from major employment centres. For a household with two working adults, the savings could instead help pay for food, rent or school expenses. The policy would therefore help household budgets while widening the area in which residents could look for work.
>
> Free fares could also encourage some drivers to change how they travel. If buses and trains cost nothing while driving still involves fuel and parking charges, public transport becomes the cheaper option. Even a modest shift could reduce the number of vehicles entering busy city centres at peak times. This outcome is not automatic, since overcrowded or infrequent services would remain unattractive, but governments could address that problem by improving capacity alongside the fare policy.
>
> In conclusion, I support free public transport as a means of reducing congestion. Its strongest effects would come from lowering the cost of commuting and giving drivers a credible alternative to using their cars.

可能的表现变化：

| 轮次 | 主要改善 | 仍需注意 |
|---|---|---|
| 原稿 | 立场和两个基本理由清楚 | 因果跳跃、过度断言、系统性语言错误 |
| 第一轮 | 论点得到限定和解释 | 段落功能仍需拉开 |
| 第二轮 | 每段有独立中心和清楚推进 | 仍需检查语言准确性 |
| 第三轮 | 任务回应、论证控制和语言准确性提高 | 实际分数仍取决于限时条件下能否独立完成 |

给学生的迁移动作：圈出每个主体段中最重要的因果箭头。如果从政策直接跳到结果，中间至少补一句“人们的行为为什么会改变”。

## 范例二：Discuss Both Views

### 题目

以下训练题为本项目自拟。

> Some people think schools should encourage competition among children, while others believe cooperation is more important. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

### 学生原稿

> There is a debate about competition and cooperation in school. Some people like competition but other people think teamwork is better. I think cooperation is more important.
>
> Competition can make students study hard because they want to win. School can give prize to the best student and everybody will try to get high mark. However, competition also makes stress and students may not help their classmates.
>
> Cooperation has many advantages. In the future almost every jobs need people to work with others. When children do group project, they learn communication, share ideas and solve problem together. It also makes the classroom friendly. For these reasons, schools should focus cooperation instead of competition.
>
> In conclusion, both have advantages, but cooperation is better for students and society.

### 初步诊断

- `TR-COVER`：两方都有提到，但竞争一方主要由一个简单原因和负面评价组成，讨论不够公平。
- `TR-POS`：作者立场清楚，不过结论中的 `both have advantages` 没有回应竞争在哪些方面有合理价值。
- `TR-DEV`：合作段列出 communication、ideas、problem solving 和 friendly classroom，却没有选择一条主线深入解释。
- `CC-ORG`：段落边界清楚，但每段内部偏向列点。
- `LR-COLL`：`give prize`、`make stress`、`focus cooperation` 等搭配不准确。
- `GRA-AGR`：`other people`、`every jobs need`、`group project`、`solve problem` 反复出现数与一致错误。

### 第一轮：只修 Task Response

先把双方最强理由说完整：

> Supporters of competition argue that clear goals and visible rewards can motivate students to practise consistently. A spelling contest or mathematics challenge, for example, gives pupils an immediate standard against which to measure their progress. Learning to cope with losing may also prepare them for selective examinations and later job applications.

> Those who favour cooperation point out that most academic and professional tasks depend on people combining different skills. Group work can teach children to explain an idea, listen to disagreement and take responsibility for one part of a shared result. In my view, these habits are useful across a wider range of situations, so cooperation should receive greater emphasis.

这一轮改变了什么：

- 没有把竞争写成明显错误的一方。
- 每一方都有机制和具体情境。
- 作者仍然选择合作，但判断建立在“适用范围更广”上，而不是简单说它更好。

### 第二轮：只修组织和论证关系

在竞争段末加入边界，在合作段解释为什么它应占更大比重：

> Competition is most useful when students compete against a clear standard rather than when schools constantly rank children against one another. Used in moderation, it can motivate effort without turning every classmate into a rival.

> Cooperation deserves more classroom time because it develops skills that individual contests cannot test. A group project succeeds only when members divide work, explain decisions and resolve disagreements. These demands resemble the way many workplaces operate, which makes cooperative learning relevant beyond a single subject or examination.

段间关系现在更清楚：竞争有特定用途，合作适用范围更广，因此作者的立场自然成立。

### 第三轮：修语言并形成完整版本

> Schools must decide whether children learn more from competing with one another or from working towards shared goals. Both approaches can be useful, but I believe cooperation should receive greater emphasis because it develops skills that students will need in a wider range of adult situations.
>
> Supporters of competition argue that clear goals and visible rewards can motivate students to practise consistently. A spelling contest or mathematics challenge, for example, gives pupils an immediate standard against which to measure their progress. The result can show a student which skills still require practice instead of leaving progress vague. Learning to cope with losing may also prepare them for selective examinations and later job applications. Competition is most useful, however, when students work towards a clear standard rather than being constantly ranked against their classmates.
>
> Cooperation deserves more classroom time because it develops skills that individual contests cannot test. A group project succeeds only when members divide work, explain their decisions and resolve disagreements. It also teaches children that their own performance may depend on whether they support other members of the group. These demands resemble the way many workplaces operate, where employees depend on colleagues with different knowledge and responsibilities. Cooperative tasks can therefore prepare children for both employment and participation in their communities.
>
> In conclusion, competition can encourage effort and help students judge their progress, especially when it is used in moderation. Nevertheless, schools should place greater weight on cooperation because communication, shared responsibility and conflict resolution remain useful long after a particular contest has ended.

这次修改刻意没有做什么：

- 没有使用“本文将讨论双方观点”作为预告句。
- 没有把合作写成没有缺点的万能方法。
- 没有为了显示词汇量而不断替换 `competition` 和 `cooperation`，因为它们是题目的核心概念。
- 没有编造研究、学校名称或百分比。

给学生的迁移动作：Discuss Both Views 写完后，分别用一句话说出双方“最有说服力的理由”。如果其中一方只能得到一句空泛陈述，说明讨论仍不平衡。
