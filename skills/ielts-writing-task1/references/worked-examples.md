## 演示 1｜Task 1 线图：同一套骨架 × 三个话题

**题目原型**：`The chart below shows {被测物} in {地点} between {年} and {年}.`

### 骨架（固定不变）

```
Introduction: The {图表名词} {动词} {信息内容} in {地点} {时间范围}.
Overview:     Overall, {最高项} was consistently the most {形容词}, whereas {最低项} {动词}.
Body 1:       {项目 A} {上升动词} {程度副词} from {数据} in {年} to {数据} in {年}, {分词} to {数据} in {年}.
              By contrast, {项目 B} {下降动词} {程度副词} {介词短语}.
Body 2:       {项目 C} {趋势动词}, {分词短语}. {项目 A} accounted for the largest share throughout the period,
              at around {数据}, which was roughly {倍数} the figure for {项目 B}.
```

### 替换槽（跨话题通用）

| 槽位 | 话题 A：能源消耗 | 话题 B：上网人数 | 话题 C：咖啡出口 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 图表名词 | line graph | bar chart | line graph |
| 信息内容 | changes in electricity consumption | the number of households with internet access | coffee export volumes |
| 被测物 | electricity consumption / the amount of electricity used | the number of households connected to the internet | coffee exports / the volume of coffee exported |
| 最高项 / 最低项 | coal / renewables | urban households / rural households | Brazil / Vietnam |
| 上升动词 | rose / climbed / surged | rose / increased steadily | grew / increased |
| 下降动词 | fell / declined / dropped | fell / dipped | declined / dropped |
| 单位 | terawatt-hours | millions of households | tonnes |

### 成品段落（话题 A 写好，话题 B / C 按槽替换即可）

**Introduction**
> The line graph illustrates changes in electricity consumption in four countries over a twenty-year period.

**Overview**
> Overall, coal remained the most widely used source of electricity throughout the period, whereas renewables accounted for the smallest share.

**Body 1**
> Coal consumption rose steadily from 120 terawatt-hours in 1990 to 210 terawatt-hours in 2010, reaching its highest level at the end of the period. By contrast, the amount of electricity generated from oil fell considerably, dropping from 90 to 45 terawatt-hours over the same twenty years.

**Body 2**
> Renewables, meanwhile, fluctuated only slightly for most of the period before rising sharply after 2005, when output climbed from 20 to 60 terawatt-hours. Although coal accounted for the largest share throughout, at around 45%, its dominance was noticeably reduced by 2010, with the figure for renewables roughly three times higher than it had been in 1990.

### ⑤ 反例诊断

**错误版本**
> There was a significant increase in coal. It increased a lot. Renewables also increased.

**病因**：
1. 三句全是"上升"，**句式无变化** → Grammatical Range 扣分；
2. 没有数字 → Task Achievement 缺证据；
3. 没有比较 → 7.0+ 的必要条件缺失；
4. `increased a lot` 是口语表达。

**处方**：`There was a significant increase in coal, which rose from 120 to 210 terawatt-hours, in contrast to oil, which fell by half over the same period.`

## 演示 2｜Task 1 流程图：一套骨架通吃

```
Introduction: The diagram illustrates the process by which {成品} is produced.
Overview:     Overall, the process consists of {数字} main stages, beginning with {起点} and culminating in {终点}.
Body 1:       First, {原料} is {过去分词} and {过去分词}. Once {原料} has been {过去分词}, it is {过去分词} and transported to {地点}.
Body 2:       At the following stage, {中间产物} is {过去分词} before being {过去分词} into {成品}. Finally, {成品} is {过去分词} and the cycle is repeated.
```

**成品（丝绸生产）**
> The diagram illustrates the process by which silk cloth is produced. Overall, the process consists of six main stages, beginning with the laying of eggs and culminating in the weaving of the finished cloth.
>
> First, eggs are laid by the moth and, once they have hatched, the larvae are fed on mulberry leaves until they are fully grown. At this point, the cocoons are collected and boiled, after which the silk threads are carefully unwound.
>
> At the following stage, the raw threads are twisted together before being dyed in large vats. The dyed silk is then woven into cloth, which is finally transported to factories for use in the manufacture of clothing.

**关键检查点**：① 现在时被动 ② 阶段数写对 ③ 顺序词不重复 ④ 不写原因不写评价。
