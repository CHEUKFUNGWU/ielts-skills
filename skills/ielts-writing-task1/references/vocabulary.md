# IELTS Task 1 语言系统 Cheat Sheet
### 趋势 · 程度 · 比较 · 流程 · 地图 ｜ 全部为"可填槽"形式

> **这个文件只讲 Task 1 的描述性语言。** Task 2 的话题词汇与论证词汇在 `../../ielts-writing-task2/references/vocabulary.md`。
>
> **为什么 Task 1 需要单独一份词汇表**：Task 1 考的不是"词汇量"，而是**同一件事换着说法说**。
> 同一个"上升"你要在一篇里写出 3 种以上不同的表达（`rose` / `climbed` / `saw an increase`），
> 同一个"占比"你要会 `account for` / `make up` / `constitute` 轮换。所以这里的组织方式不是"话题"，
> 而是**"意思"**——按你想表达的意思去查词。
>
> **符号**：`{ }` = 可替换内容；🅐/🅑/🅒 = 6.5 保底 / 7.5 主力 / 8+ 加分（越往上越要注意搭配）。

## 1. 趋势动词系统（按"上升/下降/不变/波动/峰谷"分组）

> **用法**：一篇文章里，同一个趋势词最多出现 1–2 次。写完通读一遍，把重复的换掉。

### 上升
```
基础：  rise · increase · grow · climb · go up
进阶：  jump · surge · soar · rocket · shoot up · escalate（🅒）
名词：  a rise · an increase · a growth · a climb · an upsurge · an upturn · an upward trend
```
### 下降
```
基础：  fall · decline · decrease · drop · go down · dip
进阶：  plummet · plunge · slump · tumble · dive · nosedive（🅒）
名词：  a fall · a decline · a decrease · a drop · a downturn · a reduction · a downward trend
```
### 不变
```
levelled off · plateaued · stabilised · flattened out · remained stable / steady / constant / unchanged
名词：a period of stability · little change
```
### 波动
```
fluctuated · varied (around {数值}) · oscillated（🅒）
名词：fluctuation · variation
```
### 峰谷
```
reach a peak / a high of {数值} · peak at {数值} · hit a low · bottom out at {数值} · reach its lowest point
名词：a peak of · a high of · a low of · a trough（🅒）
```
### 转变方向 / 恢复
```
overtake · surpass · exceed · recover · rebound · reverse · level out · taper off
```
⚠️ **`plummet` / `plunge` / `soar` / `rocket` / `surge` 后面不加程度副词**（本身已含"剧烈"）。

## 2. 程度与速度系统（形容词 / 副词配对表）

> **核心规则**：`动词 → 配副词`；`名词 → 配形容词`。两句意思完全相同。

| 幅度 | 形容词（+ 名词）| 副词（+ 动词）|
|---|---|---|
| 剧烈 | dramatic, sharp, steep, substantial, significant, considerable, marked | dramatically, sharply, steeply, substantially, significantly, considerably, markedly |
| 中等 | moderate, noticeable | moderately, noticeably |
| 平缓 | gradual, steady, consistent | gradually, steadily, consistently |
| 微小 | slight, marginal, minimal, negligible | slightly, marginally, minimally |
| 快速 | rapid, quick, swift | rapidly, quickly, swiftly |
| 缓慢 | slow | slowly |

**程度 + 趋势的固定搭配（背整块，不要拆）**
```
a sharp rise in {被测物}            a slight fall in {被测物}
a steady increase in {被测物}       a dramatic drop in {被测物}
a gradual decline in {被测物}       a marked improvement in {被测物}
to rise sharply                    to fall slightly
to increase steadily               to decline gradually
```

## 3. 同一意思的四种说法（句式轮换表）

> 这是 Task 1 拿语法分的关键：**同一个数据点，用四种句式各写一遍。**

```
① there be 型：   There was a significant increase in {X} from {A} to {B}.
② 名词+动词型：   {X} increased significantly from {A} to {B}.
③ 时间作主语型：  {年份} saw a significant increase in {X}, from {A} to {B}.
④ 经历型：        {X} experienced a significant increase, rising from {A} to {B}.
⑤ 分词收尾型：    {X} increased significantly, reaching {B} in {年份}.
```
**降级/升级对照（同一个意思，三个档次）**
```
🅐  {X} went up a lot.
🅑  There was a significant increase in {X}.
🅒  {X} rose sharply, reaching its highest level of the period at {数据}.
```

## 4. 份额与占比语言

```
占比动词：account for ｜ make up ｜ constitute ｜ represent ｜ comprise ｜ take up ｜ form
名词短语：the proportion of {人} who {动词} ｜ the percentage of {群体} ｜ the share of {项目}
          the figure for {项目} ｜ {项目}'s share of the total
```
**模板**
```
{项目} accounted for {数字}% of {总量}, making it the largest / smallest {类别名词}.
The proportion of {项目} stood at {数字}%, compared with just {数字}% for {项目 2}.
{A} made up roughly half / a third / a quarter of {总量}.
```
**模糊份额（数字太密时用）**
```
just under / just over {数字}%       around / approximately / roughly {数字}%
slightly more than half              fewer than one in five
a mere {数字}%                       as much as {数字}%
```
⚠️ `comprise` 后面**不加 of**：`X comprises 30% of the total` ✓ ｜ `comprises of` ✗

## 5. 比较与倍数语言

```
最高级：   {A} was the most {形容词} {类别} {范围}.
           {A} was more {形容词} than any other {类别}.
比较级：   {A} was more {形容词} than {B}.
           {A} was more {形容词} among {群体 1} than {群体 2}.
倍数：     {A} was twice / three times as high as {B}.
           {A} was three times the figure for {B}.
           The figure for {A} was {数字} times higher than that for {B}.
相似：     The figure for {A} was almost identical to that for {B}.
           {A} and {B} were broadly similar, at {数据} and {数据} respectively.
          A higher percentage of {群体 1} than {群体 2} {动词}.
对比：     Compared with / to {B}, {A} was considerably higher.
           In contrast to {A}, {B} {动词短语}.
           {A} {动词}, whereas {B} {动词}.
           While {A} {动词}, {B} {动词}.
差距：     The gap between {A} and {B} widened / narrowed over the period.
           The difference was particularly pronounced in {维度}.
```
**连接词选择规则（高频错点）**

| 连接词 | 后面接 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| `whereas` / `while` | **完整从句** | X rose, **whereas** Y fell. |
| `compared with / to` | **名词短语** | **Compared with** Y, X was higher. |
| `despite / in spite of` | **名词短语** | **Despite** the rise in X, Y fell. |
| `although / though` | **完整从句** | **Although** X rose, Y fell. |

`respectively`（分别，按前文顺序）｜ `in turn`（依次、相应地）｜ `the former / the latter`（前者 / 后者，仅在两项且指代清楚时用）

## 6. 近似与估计语言

```
just over / just under          more than / less than
nearly / almost / very nearly   about / around / approximately / roughly
just about                      marginally above / below
well over / well below          in the region of {数据}
```
**用法**：overview 里不放数字；主体段里数字密集时用近似词收拢，例如
`Spending on {项目} stood at around {数据}, slightly more than the figure for {项目 2}.`

## 7. 时间表达与数字介词

```
时间范围
  from {年} to {年}              between {年} and {年}
  over the period from {年} to {年}   during / throughout the period from {年} to {年}
  over a {数字}-year period        over the next {数字} years
  during the first / last two years   for the following five years
  in the first / last three months of the year   for the rest of the year
  at the beginning / end of the period            over a ten-year period
顺序
  after that / then / subsequently / following this / thereafter / until / up to / by
介词与数字
  in 2000（在某年）        by 1995（到某年为止）      at 15%（处于某值）
  fell to 20%（降到的终点）  fell by 25%（降的幅度）
  from 45% in 1998 to 20% in 2010
  an increase of 10% in the number of {被测物}
  rose from £20 to £35, an increase of 75%
```

## 8. 起点 / 终点 / 定位语言

```
stood at {数据}        （静态定位，万能主语是 the figure for {项目}）
started / began at {数据}
ended / finished at {数据}
was / reached / recorded / totalled / amounted to {数据}
accounted for {数据} of {总量}
```
**替换菜单**：`stood at → was / reached / recorded / totalled`
**模板**：`In {年}, {被测物} stood at {数据} before {分词短语}.`
**救命句**：想不起怎么措辞时，主语直接用 `The figure for {项目}` —— 口诀："The figure for X works all the time."

## 9. 预测与未来语言

```
{X} is expected to {动词} to {数据} by {年}.
{X} is predicted / projected to {动词} {程度副词} over the next {数字} years.
{X} is likely to {动词} {时间}.
By {年}, {X} will have {过去分词} to {数据}.          （🅒 将来完成时）
{X} is set to {动词} {时间}.                          （🅒 地道）
If current trends continue, {X} will {动词} by {年}.   （🅒 全篇只用一次）
```
```
动词槽：rise / fall / reach / exceed / double / halve / overtake
```

## 10. 流程图动词库（被动语态）

```
收集运输：collected · gathered · transported · delivered · shipped · distributed

成形包装：packaged · labelled · sealed · stored · stacked · wrapped
转化废弃：converted into · transformed into · turned into · removed · discarded · recycled
添加移除：added · combined with · introduced to · extracted from · removed from
```
**顺序连接词库（变换使用，别只用 first/then）**
```

Once … / After … / Before … / Meanwhile

```
**模板**

## 12. 通用措辞替换表（动词 / 名词 / 图表名词 / 被测物）

**图表名词与动词**
```
图表：  chart · graph · line graph · bar chart · pie chart · table · diagram · figure · illustration
动词：  shows · illustrates · depicts · indicates · reveals · compares · outlines
        gives information about · provides data on · presents · displays
```

**名词化（把题目原句压成名词短语）**
```
the number of people who visited {地点}   → visitor numbers / the number of visitors
spending on {项目}                        → expenditure on {项目}
the production of coffee                  → coffee production
the amount of money made                  → revenue / income / turnover
the level of unemployment                 → the unemployment rate
the quality of food in supermarkets       → supermarket food quality
investment in research                    → research investment
```

**同义替换表**
```
sales = income = revenue = turnover = how much money was made
proportion = percentage = rate = share
the proportion of = the percentage of = the figure for
from 2000 to 2005 = between 2000 and 2005 = over a five-year period
the elderly = elderly people = senior citizens
spending = expenditure        information = data
production = manufacture = output      to produce = to make = to manufacture
increase (v) = rise = grow     decrease (v) = fall = decline = drop
```

## 13. Task 1 用词雷区（别用或慎用）

### 13.1 直接不要用

```
kids → children                a lot of → many / a great deal of
things → （换具体名词）          stuff → （换具体名词）
don't / can't / it's → do not / cannot / it is
! ? → .（Task 1 是陈述性报告）
Nowadays（句首）→ In recent years / At present
In conclusion → （Task 1 没有结论段，总结写进 Overview）
I think / In my opinion → （Task 1 不表观点，删掉）
This is because / due to → （Task 1 不解释原因，删掉）
```
### 13.2 慎用（搭配难或语气过重）

| 词 | 问题 | 更安全的选择 |
|---|---|---|
| `plummet / plunge / rocket / soar / surge` | 本身含"剧烈"，**再加程度副词就是搭配错误** | 单独使用；或改 `fell sharply` |
| `enhance` | 被 band 9 范文作者点名为"最常被误用的词"，偏广告语域 | improve / strengthen |
| `efficiency` | 该用 effectiveness 时别用 efficiency | effectiveness |
| `popular` 描述趋势 | 要求改用 `common` | `the most common {类别}` |
| `utilise` | 显得刻意 | use |
| `dramatically` | 用滥了，且和 `plummet` 类词冲突 | sharply / substantially（或换词）|
| `approximately` | 全篇出现 3 次以上就显得机械 | 轮换 roughly / around / just over |
| `show` | 开头一句用一次即可 | illustrates / depicts / compares |

### 13.3 搭配炸弹（错一个就明显）

```
✅ an increase IN {被测物}        ✗ an increase of {被测物}
✅ a rise OF 10%                  ✗ a rise in 10%
✅ fell TO 20%（终点）            ✗ fell by 20%（那是幅度）
✅ fell BY 25%（幅度）
✅ the number of students WAS     ✗ the number of students WERE
✅ account for 30%                ✗ account 30%
✅ comprise 30% of the total      ✗ comprise of 30%
✅ the proportion of students     ✗ the proportion of student
✅ 20% 不加空格                    ✅ £20 / $15（货币符号后不加空格）
```
### 13.4 英式 / 美式拼写（全篇统一）

```
英式（推荐）：programme · centre · metre · labour · analyse · organisation · travelled
美式：        program · center · meter · labor · analyze · organization · traveled
```
