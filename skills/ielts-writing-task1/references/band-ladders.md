# Task 1 阶梯式修改范例

本文件展示怎样从学生原稿逐步修改，而不是直接换成另一篇范文。分数只表示可能区间，目的是说明评分项怎样变化，不是给孤立文本颁发官方成绩。

使用时只读取与当前题型对应的一组。每一轮只处理一个主要层面：

1. Task Achievement：信息是否准确、overview 是否清楚、关键特征是否得到支持。
2. Coherence & Cohesion：信息怎样分组，比较或步骤是否容易跟随。
3. Lexical Resource 与 Grammatical Range & Accuracy：修正语言，同时保留学生原意和可控制的句子复杂度。

## 范例一：动态线图

### 题目与数据

以下训练题为本项目自拟，不对应真实考试题。

> The line graph shows the amount of electricity generated from coal, gas and renewable sources in Norland in 2000, 2010 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

单位为 terawatt-hours：

| 来源 | 2000 | 2010 | 2020 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Coal | 70 | 60 | 35 |
| Gas | 30 | 45 | 50 |
| Renewables | 10 | 25 | 55 |

### 学生原稿

> The line graph give information about three kinds of electricity in Norland from 2000 to 2020. Overall, all source changed considerably. Coal was the highest at first and renewable increased.
>
> In 2000 coal produced 70 terawatt hours, which was much more higher than gas at 30. Renewable energy only had 10. After ten years, coal decreased 10 and gas rose to 55. Renewable also went up to 25.
>
> In 2020, renewable became 55 and gas was 50. Coal had a big decrease to 35. This show that clean energy became popular in the country.

### 初步诊断

如果其余表现相近，这份原稿可能受到以下限制：

- `TA-OV`：overview 只说“有变化”，没有概括煤炭下降、可再生能源跃居第一以及能源结构逆转。
- `TA-DATA`：2010 年 gas 被写成 55，原数据是 45。
- `TA-SEL`：结尾解释“clean energy became popular”，超出了图表提供的信息。
- `LR-COLL`：`renewable became 55`、`had a big decrease` 不自然。
- `GRA-AGR`：`graph give`、`all source`、`This show` 重复反映一致问题。
- 全文不足 150 词，提供的任务和语言证据有限。

这不是“改几个高级词”能解决的问题。先修数据和 overview。

### 第一轮：只修 Task Achievement

把开头改为：

> The line graph compares electricity generation from coal, gas and renewable sources in Norland in 2000, 2010 and 2020. Overall, coal was the dominant source at the beginning but declined throughout the period. By contrast, renewables grew fastest and became the largest source by 2020, while gas rose more moderately.

把错误数据和无依据推测改为：

> In 2010, coal generation had fallen to 60 terawatt-hours, while gas and renewables had risen to 45 and 25 respectively.
>
> By 2020, renewables had reached 55 terawatt-hours, five more than gas, whereas coal had dropped to 35.

这一轮改变了什么：

- overview 现在覆盖两条最重要的变化。
- 2010 年 gas 数字得到纠正。
- 删除“became popular”，因为图表只显示发电量，不显示公众态度。
- 学生原来的中心观察仍然保留：煤炭下降，可再生能源上升。

### 第二轮：只修信息组织

原稿按年份逐项罗列。改为按趋势分组：

- Body 1 写下降的 coal，并把它与起点较低但持续上升的 gas 比较。
- Body 2 写 renewables 的快速增长，再突出 2020 年的最终排序。

修改后的主体段：

> Coal began at 70 terawatt-hours in 2000, more than twice the figure for gas, at 30. Over the next two decades, coal generation fell to 60 and then 35, while gas followed the opposite pattern, rising to 45 in 2010 and 50 in 2020. The gap between these two sources therefore narrowed from 40 terawatt-hours to 15 in 2010 before gas moved ahead.
>
> Renewables started at only 10 terawatt-hours, the lowest of the three sources. Their output then increased to 25 in 2010 before more than doubling to 55 by 2020. As a result, renewables finished the period five terawatt-hours above gas and 20 above coal.

这一轮没有做什么：

- 没有强行增加更多数据。
- 没有加入原因、评价或政策建议。
- 没有为了四段式而拆散本来清楚的信息组。

### 第三轮：修语言并形成完整版本

> The line graph compares the amount of electricity generated from coal, gas and renewable sources in Norland in 2000, 2010 and 2020.
>
> Overall, coal was the dominant source at the beginning but declined throughout the period. By contrast, renewables grew fastest and became the largest source by 2020, while gas rose more moderately.
>
> Coal began at 70 terawatt-hours in 2000, more than twice the figure for gas, at 30. Over the next two decades, coal generation fell to 60 and then 35, while gas followed the opposite pattern, rising to 45 in 2010 and 50 in 2020. The gap between these two sources therefore narrowed from 40 terawatt-hours to 15 in 2010 before gas moved ahead.
>
> Renewables started at only 10 terawatt-hours, the lowest of the three sources. Their output then increased to 25 in 2010 before more than doubling to 55 by 2020. As a result, renewables finished the period five terawatt-hours above gas and 20 above coal.

可能的表现变化：

| 轮次 | 主要改善 | 仍需注意 |
|---|---|---|
| 原稿 | 能识别大致趋势 | 数据错误、overview 不充分、语言错误重复、字数不足 |
| 第一轮 | 关键特征和数据准确 | 主体仍可能像逐项报告 |
| 第二轮 | 分组和比较更清楚 | 仍需检查语言控制 |
| 第三轮 | 准确性、比较语言和句式控制提高 | 实际分数仍要结合完整限时表现判断 |

给学生的迁移动作：写完 overview 后，遮住图表，检查它是否仍能告诉读者“谁总体上升、谁下降、最终排序怎样”。

## 范例二：纸张回收流程图

### 题目与流程

以下训练题为本项目自拟。

> The diagram shows how used office paper is recycled into new paper. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features.

流程共有六步：收集废纸；分类并移除不适合回收的纸张；加水和化学品制浆；清除油墨和胶水；压制以除去多余水分并烘干；卷成新纸卷。

### 学生原稿

> The picture shows how people recycle papers. Overall, it is a long process and many machines are used.
>
> First, workers collect used papers from offices and it will sort into different groups. Next, they put water and chemical to the paper for making pulp. After this, the machine clean the ink and glue. The pulp is dried and press, then new paper is rolled. Finally, it can use again by office workers.

### 初步诊断

- `TA-OV`：“a long process”没有说明起点、终点或主要转变。
- `TA-DATA`：原稿把 drying 放在 pressing 前面，次序与图示不符。
- `CC-LINK`：步骤能大致跟随，但主语在 people、workers、they、machine 之间跳动。
- `LR-COLL`：`recycle papers`、`put water and chemical to`、`for making pulp` 不自然。
- `GRA-V`：被动结构反复出错，如 `will sort`、`is dried and press`、`can use`。
- 字数不足 150，细节覆盖有限。

### 第一轮：只修 Task Achievement

新的 overview：

> Overall, the process consists of six stages, beginning with the collection and sorting of used office paper and ending with the production of rolls of recycled paper. The main transformation occurs when the sorted paper is turned into pulp and cleaned before being pressed and dried.

纠正步骤顺序：

> After the ink and glue have been removed, the clean pulp is pressed into sheets and then dried. The finished paper is finally wound into rolls.

这一轮只确保流程完整、顺序正确。没有要求每一步都换一个罕见动词。

### 第二轮：只修顺序和指代

把主体分为两个阶段：

- 前半段：废纸进入系统并变成干净纸浆。
- 后半段：纸浆变成纸张和纸卷。

修改后的主体：

> First, used office paper is collected and sorted according to its type and quality, while unsuitable material is removed. The selected paper is then mixed with water and chemicals to form pulp. At the next stage, this mixture passes through cleaning equipment, which removes ink and glue.
>
> Once the pulp has been cleaned, it is pressed into continuous sheets, which removes much of the remaining water. A separate drying stage then removes the moisture left after pressing. The finished paper is finally wound into large rolls, ready to be used again in offices.

### 第三轮：修语言并形成完整版本

> The diagram illustrates how used office paper is recycled to produce new paper.
>
> Overall, the process consists of six stages, beginning with the collection and sorting of used paper and ending with the production of rolls of recycled paper. The main transformation occurs when the sorted material is turned into pulp and cleaned before being pressed and dried.
>
> First, used office paper is collected and sorted according to its type and quality, while unsuitable material is removed. The selected paper is then mixed with water and chemicals to form pulp. At the next stage, this mixture passes through cleaning equipment, which removes ink and glue.
>
> Once the pulp has been cleaned, it is pressed into continuous sheets, which removes much of the remaining water. A separate drying stage then removes the moisture left after pressing. The finished paper is finally wound into large rolls, ready to be used again in offices.

这次修改保留了什么：

- 保留原稿的六步信息，没有增加图中不存在的运输、包装或销售阶段。
- 保留简单的顺序表达，只减少重复和含糊指代。
- 被动语态用于流程对象，但没有为了语法范围强行加入条件句或倒装。

给学生的迁移动作：写完流程图后，把每个动词按顺序列出来，逐一核对图示，再检查每一步的主语是流程对象还是执行者。
